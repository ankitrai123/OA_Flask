      Prep Quantity — Newsvendor Model

      How much to prepare, given the cost of running out vs. the cost of spoilage

      What if demand or costs change?

      Critical Ratio = Cu / (Cu + Co)

      Cu (understage/stockout cost) is the contribution margin lost when demand exceeds what was prepared. Co (overage/spoilage cost) is the unit cost of an item prepared but not sold (salvage value is ₹0 in this data). The optimal prep quantity is the CR-th quantile of the demand distribution — for a point forecast with uncertainty (mean, std), this is mean + std × Φ⁻¹(CR), the standard newsvendor closed form under a Normal approximation.
