      Model Validation — Rolling-Origin vs Naive 80/20 Split

      FoldCutoffARIMAX RMSENaive RMSE
      Why rolling-origin, not a single split? A single 80/20 train/test split can land anywhere in the calendar - if the held-out window happens to miss the summer months, it understates the model's real value. 5-fold rolling-origin validation, sampled across the full 2-year history, is the headline result for exactly that reason.
